Title: Applying an existing payment to an unpaid invoice

Issue:
An invoice remains unpaid even though a payment has already been received.

Keywords:
payment, invoice, unpaid invoice, payment allocation, payment application, receipt, financial account, accounts receivable

Problem:
A payment has been successfully recorded, but it has not been associated with the invoice that remains unpaid.

Expected:
The payment is associated with the appropriate invoice and the invoice reflects a Paid status.

Actual:
The account balance may be correct, but the invoice continues to display as unpaid.

Cause:
The payment has not been applied or allocated to the fee or transaction associated with the invoice.

How To:
1. Open the customer or financial account associated with the payment.
2. Locate the existing payment or receipt.
3. Review the payment application or allocation details.
4. Select the unpaid fee, charge, or invoice that should receive the payment.
5. Save or confirm the payment allocation.
6. Verify the invoice now reflects the payment and displays the appropriate status.

Resolution:
Applying the payment to the correct fee or transaction associates it with the invoice and updates the invoice status.

Notes:
- Verify the correct invoice before applying the payment.
- Ensure the payment has not already been applied to another transaction.
- Confirm the account balance and invoice status after saving the changes.
